# 💫 About Me

Hi! I’m Laura 👋

I’m a Senior Designer with 15 years of experience across visual design, brand systems, editorial, print production, web, and digital projects.

Over time, my work has naturally expanded beyond creating individual outputs into understanding the systems behind them: how information is structured, how visual decisions become reusable rules, how projects move from concept to production, and how repetitive work can be improved.

Today, I’m especially interested in creative workflows, editorial and production systems, AI-assisted processes, and practical ways to connect design with technology.

I use this space to experiment with web projects, workflow tools, prototypes, automation ideas, and other things that help make creative processes clearer, more useful, and less repetitive.

Design first. Technology where it helps. Human judgment throughout.

Feel free to connect: **hello@lauramujica.com**

Have a great day!  
Laura ⚡
